15-day replacement if the plant arrives unhealthy.

Grown for eighteen months in our own greenhouse, this plant arrives established rather than freshly potted — which means far less transplant stress and a much better survival rate in its first month with you.

The pot is thrown from reclaimed clay by a small studio two hours from our nursery. Every one is slightly different, which we think is the point.

  • Light: bright indirect. Avoid harsh afternoon sun.
  • Water: when the top 2cm of soil is dry, roughly weekly.
  • Humidity: loves a misting; happiest above 50%.
  • Feed: diluted liquid feed once a month from March to September.
